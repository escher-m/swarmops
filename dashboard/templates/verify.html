What this check actually does Five independent checks, each catching a different attack
Hash integrity
Each record's stored hash is recomputed from its content. Catches edits to any field.
Chain linkage
Each record's prev_record_hash must equal its predecessor's hash. Catches deletion and reordering, which per-record hashing alone would miss.
Sequence density
Sequence numbers must be contiguous. A gap means a row was removed by someone who could also fix the links.
Schema validity
Every record must still satisfy the published schema.
Evidence integrity
Every referenced blob must exist and still hash to its address. Catches swapping the log excerpt that justified an action while leaving the record untouched.
